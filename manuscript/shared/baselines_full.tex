\emph{AODV} \cite{perkins1999aodv}: reactive discovery with RREQ flooding (24-byte frames, TTL 4), RREP from the gateway or from an intermediate node with a fresh route, 3\,s active-route timeout, 150\,ms discovery timer with two retries, RERR on link failure and FIFO buffering during discovery (RFC~3561 defaults scaled to the 13-node network).
\emph{M-ATTEMPT} \cite{javaid2013mattempt}: every 5\,s each node selects the minimum-hop parent with the lowest energy-cost route (linear path loss plus the parent's advertised cost); critical data goes directly to the gateway; a failed parent triggers direct transmission until the next round.
\emph{SIMPLE} \cite{nadeem2013simple}: every 5\,s the gateway elects the non-critical node minimising $d(i,g)/E_{\mathrm{res}}(i)$ as forwarder and broadcasts it; non-critical nodes closer to the forwarder than to the gateway use it, others and critical nodes transmit directly.
\emph{ACO-WBAN}: an AntHocNet-style scheme \cite{dicaro2005anthocnet} in which each sensor emits a forward ant every second that follows the pheromone table stochastically ($\beta=1$); the backward ant returned by the gateway updates pheromone along the path with the path-goodness estimate $\tau=1/(\hat T+h\,T_{\mathrm{hop}})$ and $\gamma=0.7$; data frames follow $\propto\tau^{\beta}$ with $\beta=5$ and a failed hop evaporates the entry by 50\%.
\emph{PSO-Relay} and \emph{GWO-Relay}: every 5\,s the gateway optimises the relay assignment of the non-critical sensors (option set: gateway or any non-critical node not farther in hops) with 20 particles/wolves and 40 iterations of standard PSO (inertia 0.9$\to$0.2, $c_1=c_2=1.5$) or GWO \cite{mirjalili2014gwo}, minimising a weighted sum of the worst energy drain rate, total transmit energy and expected loss computed from reported energies and the mean CM3 path loss, as in PSOBAN \cite{bilandi2019psoban} and \cite{bilandi2020performance}; the assignment is broadcast in a 40-byte frame; critical sensors transmit directly and never relay.
\emph{QL-WBAN}: Q-routing \cite{boyan1994qrouting} with per-neighbour cost estimates initialised from hop counts, update $Q_{i}(j)\leftarrow Q_{i}(j)+\eta\,(q+s+t_{j}-Q_{i}(j))$ with $\eta=0.5$, where $q+s$ is the measured queueing plus service time and $t_{j}$ the best estimate piggybacked by $j$ in its ACK, an energy penalty $\zeta(1-R_{j})$ with $\zeta=5$\,ms, a 50\,ms penalty on hop failure and $\epsilon$-greedy selection with $\epsilon=0.05$, in the spirit of \cite{guo2022rlfuzzywban,arafat2024rlwban}.
\emph{QoS-2C}: a two-class scheme after DMQoS/QPRD/ZEQoS \cite{razzaque2011dmqos,khan2012qprd,khan2014zeqos}: critical frames take the neighbour with the smallest advertised path delay among those with link reliability $\ge 0.9$ (else the most reliable), ordinary frames the closer-to-gateway neighbour minimising $d_{ij}^{2}/E_{\mathrm{res}}(j)$; reliability is an EWMA of ACK outcomes ($\beta=0.4$) and path delays are advertised in beacons.
